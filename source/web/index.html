Media engine & codecs

Built-in FFmpeg engine
Checking…
 bundled
Included with Lumen — no codec pack or separate install is required.
Containers: MKV, MP4/MOV, WebM, AVI, MPEG-TS/M2TS, FLV, VOB, WMV/ASF, Ogg, MXF and more.
Video: H.264, HEVC/H.265 (x265, 10-bit), AV1, VP9/VP8, MPEG-1/2/4, VC-1, WMV, ProRes, Theora and many legacy codecs.
Audio: AAC, MP3, FLAC, Opus/Vorbis, AC-3/E-AC-3, DTS, TrueHD, WMA, ALAC, PCM and more.
When Chromium cannot decode a track, Lumen converts it to H.264/AAC during playback. High-resolution conversion uses CPU.
